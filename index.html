What is Information Technology?

IT is the use of computer systems and devices to access information. It means using technology to organize, store, share and process information.

Organize

Sort files into folders or customers into a database.

Store

Keep photos on your phone or in the cloud.

Share

Send an email or a WhatsApp message in seconds.

Process

Calculate salaries or totals in Excel.

What belongs to IT? Tap a word.

The IT industry grows fast because companies depend more and more on technology, and managing information is vital.

What is a computer?

A computer is an electronic device that stores and processes data in binary, following instructions called a program. It is hardware (physical parts) plus software (instructions).

1. Input

Takes in data and instructions.

2. Processing

Works with the data.

3. Output

The CPU

The Central Processing Unit is the brain of the computer. It does arithmetic and logic, controls other hardware, and repeats the instruction cycle non-stop.

Software

Software is a set of instructions that tells the computer what to do. Hardware is what you can touch.

Hardware

Physical parts made of circuits and transistors: CPU, RAM, screen.

System software

Runs the computer itself: Windows, Linux, macOS, Android.

Application software

Helps you do a task: MS Word, Excel, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram.

Binary: the language of computers

Electronic circuits have two states, so computers use two digits: 0 and 1.

ON = 1

A positive voltage, for example 5 V.

OFF = 0

No voltage: 0 V.

Many on/off states

A series of them can represent any information.
